# Try the public beta

Career Vault is an early AI skill. You can try it without coding or installation using the README's text upload/copy path. Onboarding guides cover Claude and ChatGPT, but this release has not yet been tested live in those accounts.

## A useful first test

Use your own AI account and a private storage destination you choose. Read the privacy notice first. You can start with the [fictional walkthrough](demo.md) if you want to explore without sharing career information.

1. Begin with an existing resume or project description. Check that goals come first and questions feel manageable.
2. Explore the last three companies (or all if fewer); pause or defer one area. Notice whether quieter contributions emerge and answered questions repeat.
3. Review proposed positioning and accomplishment claims. Correct a detail before approving outputs.
4. Produce a resume and relevant case study, then a presentation outline from the same evidence. Check individual/team attribution and unsupported claims.
5. Save a full Markdown record in your own private location. Open a fresh conversation or different assistant, provide the skill and record, and resume. Check preferences, fuller notes and unfinished questions survive.
6. Change the goal or add a forgotten project. Confirm cross-material updates are proposed for review.

You can stop at any time. No sensitive employer document is required. The skill itself collects no feedback or career data; your AI provider processes what you share under its settings.

## Share feedback safely

[Open a beta feedback issue](https://github.com/daibot/career-vault/issues/new?template=feedback.md). Issues are public. Describe the behavior with synthetic or carefully redacted inputs. Do not attach personal career records, resumes, client files, credentials or identifying screenshots.

Useful details include app/model/date if known, how you loaded the skill, what you tried, what happened, what you expected and a short fictional reproduction. Include positives too: which question or output helped, described without personal facts.

## Priorities for the next version

Live setup on both hosts; actual checkpoint export/import; typing-only and voice preferences; fuller company/project discovery; factual attribution and claim correction. Invented claims, unwanted data transmission or lost approval history should be addressed before claims of broader reliability. A small beta does not establish hiring effectiveness.

Public source is available, but no outreach messages are sent automatically and no participant information is collected by the skill.
