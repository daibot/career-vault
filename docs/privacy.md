# Privacy in plain language

Career Vault is an AI skill distributed as text instructions and templates. It has no hosted application, analytics, tracking pixel, form, creator inbox, background service or career-data upload code. It collects no personal information itself, sends none to its creator, and adds no separate collection or transmission.

Using an AI assistant still means your AI provider processes the conversation and any files you provide under its own privacy, retention and workspace settings. Career Vault cannot change those settings or promise provider-level confidentiality. The name “Vault” does not mean encryption, access control or automatic backup is provided.

You choose the record's storage destination. A private local Markdown file is the proposed default. Cloud documents, shared chats, uploads, downloads and integrations have their own access and retention behavior. The playbook must not choose an external destination or publish career data on your behalf. Reading a public portfolio through host tools differs from sending your career record to a third party; do not insert private details into public searches.

Share only what you are comfortable sharing. Use aliases, remove client-confidential details and avoid unnecessary identifiers. Never put passwords or access tokens in the record. Personal records belong outside this repository; ignore rules are only a backstop, not a privacy guarantee.

## Public beta feedback

Do not send full career records, resumes, screenshots with identifiers or client files in repository issues or feedback. A synthetic reproduction is enough: what you asked, the behavior, what you expected, and the app/model/date if you know them. Everyone should keep their records in their own chosen storage, not in this repository. No automatic feedback is collected.

## Removing data

Delete your copies and use your provider's data controls for conversations/uploads and any chosen storage service. Career Vault has no independent database to delete from. Deleting a local copy does not delete provider-held data or copies already shared elsewhere.
