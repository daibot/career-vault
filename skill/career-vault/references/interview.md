# Interview map

Use this while interviewing, with the record template alongside it. Ask the next useful question, never the entire list. Mark each field `known`, `missing`, `uncertain`, `needs-clarification` or `deferred`; add evidence/source and whether it is essential for the current deliverable or optional later. Known does not mean approved for public use.

## Begin with what exists

After the first goal response, establish the user's preferred mode and storage destination only where unanswered. Invite existing materials. Reflect a short factual understanding and uncertainties; ask one correction question. Establish company chronology so “last three” is grounded in the person's history rather than the order files were uploaded. Record concurrent roles and uncertain dates without inventing an order.

## Company coverage

Repeat for the last three companies, or all if fewer. A role change inside one company does not count as another company.

| Field | Useful opening or follow-up |
| --- | --- |
| Context and hiring purpose | What problem were you brought in to solve? |
| Roles/promotions and dates | How did your responsibilities change while you were there? |
| Actual scope | What did you handle that your title did not capture? |
| Projects/challenges, including quieter work | What else did you make possible that rarely shows up in your portfolio? |
| Individual decisions and actions | What did you personally decide or change? |
| Team boundary | Which parts belonged to teammates or partners? |
| Leadership/collaboration | When did people depend on your judgment or guidance? |
| Value | Who benefited, and what became better, easier or less risky? |
| Outcomes/evidence | What happened afterward that you can point to? |
| Feedback and routine strengths | What did people repeatedly ask you for help with? |
| Reflection and approval | Here is what I think your contribution was. What needs correcting? |

For each substantial project, deepen the account: starting conditions, constraints/tradeoffs, alternatives considered, personal action, collaborators, observed outcome, evidence and limits. If a user says “I just made the decks,” ask what decisions those decks enabled. If they say “we launched,” ask their part without diminishing collaborative work. If numbers are absent, seek specific qualitative observations; never propose invented percentages.

When an answer covers several fields, map it once and only follow up on gaps. Capture a tangent under the right company/project, note the suspended question and return after the productive thread. Do not reset coverage when a new project appears. Ask whether another meaningful project or less visible contribution belongs in this company before the company reflection.

## Exploration and assessment

Investigate transferable capabilities, energizing work/interests, unwanted tasks or environments, and practical constraints such as location, compensation needs, schedule, retraining time and appetite for change. Ask only what is relevant; uncertainty and “prefer not to say” are valid. Show two or three possible directions with supporting claims and what to investigate next. Do not turn a preference into evidence of capability.

An assessment should link each proposed strength to claim IDs, explain why it serves the goal, flag emphasis that obscures stronger evidence and distinguish narrative problems from actual experience gaps. Example: “Your mentoring is evidenced, but managing budgets is not yet established. For this role, lead with mentoring and investigate the budget gap rather than claiming department ownership.” Ask one approval/correction question before drafting.

## Stopping and resuming

Show progress on request in a compact company/field/status/gap table. For an urgent output prioritize essential gaps and make limitations visible. The user may defer any interview area. Checkpoint before stopping: full record, approvals/disputes, chosen storage, pending gaps, paused tangent and next question. On return, read it and continue at that question unless the goal changed. A checkpoint prepared in chat is not a saved file.
