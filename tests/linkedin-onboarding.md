# LinkedIn onboarding evaluation

These are agent simulations, not a live cross-host test. No external sources were accessed. The URL is fictional; no real career details or personal record paths are included.

## Original baseline request

Evaluate the existing public skill before an onboarding change. Synthetic user: “I want to explore my next career move, typing is fine, and I will copy the record from chat. I have not supplied any materials yet.” Produce the next user-facing response inviting an existing source, at most 120 words. Report whether a LinkedIn profile URL was explicitly offered. Do not write files or access external sources.

## Before: exact baseline response

> Career Vault adds no separate collection or transmission of your information; your AI provider processes this chat under its own privacy settings. You choose what to share.
>
> We’ll explore your next career move through typing, and I’ll keep the career record ready for you to copy from chat.
>
> Could you share an existing résumé, case study, portfolio URL or work sample as a starting point? Share whichever is easiest, with any sensitive details removed.

Baseline assessment: LinkedIn profile URL explicitly offered: **No**.

Exact source invitation before:

> Could you share an existing résumé, case study, portfolio URL or work sample as a starting point? Share whichever is easiest, with any sensitive details removed.

## After: simulation A

Input: “I want to explore my next career move, typing is fine, and I will copy the record from chat. I have not supplied any materials yet.”

Actual simulated user-facing response (under 120 words):

> Career Vault adds no separate collection or transmission of your information; your AI provider processes this chat under its own privacy settings. You choose what to share.
>
> We’ll explore your next career move through typing, with a full Markdown career record ready to copy from chat. Chat memory is not a durable backup.
>
> Could you share one existing source—a résumé, LinkedIn profile URL, portfolio URL, case study or work sample—whichever is easiest? You can remove sensitive details first.

Exact source invitation after:

> Could you share one existing source—a résumé, LinkedIn profile URL, portfolio URL, case study or work sample—whichever is easiest? You can remove sensitive details first.

## After: simulation B

Input: “Here is my fictional LinkedIn profile URL https://www.linkedin.com/in/example-creative/ . Please start from it.”

Host fixture: no authorized browser or browsing tools are available. This simulation independently applies that fixture; no browsing was attempted.

Actual simulated user-facing response (under 160 words):

> I have the URL, but I haven’t opened or read the profile: this host has no authorized browsing capability.
>
> Could you paste the profile text here, or supply a profile export you choose to share? Start with whichever is easiest, and remove sensitive details if you wish. Please don’t share passwords, session cookies or authentication tokens.
>
> I’ll treat any titles, dates, accomplishments and metrics you supply as profile claims, not independently verified facts, and reflect my initial understanding for your correction. Sharing a URL or profile text does not authorize account access or profile edits.

## Results

| Criterion | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit LinkedIn source invitation | Pass after; absent before | A names “LinkedIn profile URL.” |
| One manageable question | Pass in A and B | A requests one source of the user's choice; B requests profile content through one of two alternative formats. |
| No claimed access | Pass in B | Explicitly says the profile was not opened or read; reports the host limitation. |
| Credential handling | Pass in B | Does not request credentials and explicitly excludes passwords, cookies and tokens. |
| No account-edit authorization | Pass in B | Explicitly separates supplying content from account access or profile edits. |
| Profile claims remain unverified | Pass in B, prospective only | Describes how future supplied claims will be treated; no profile contents were supplied or inferred. |
| Length limits | Pass | A is below 120 words; B is below 160 words. |
| Typing and copy preference | Pass in A | A acknowledges typing and copyable Markdown without suggesting voice. |

## Concrete issues and limits

- The original source invitation omitted LinkedIn explicitly; the updated invitation resolves that issue in this simulation.
- No new failure was observed for these two simulated turns. B cannot establish whether source claims would actually remain labeled and unresolved ambiguities preserved later, because no profile content was supplied.
- These responses were generated by an agent that read the skill. They do not establish repeatability across models, tool implementations or hosts.
- No live cross-host test, authenticated LinkedIn read, export ingestion, partial-profile handling or profile editing was performed. Authorized-browser behavior remains untested.
